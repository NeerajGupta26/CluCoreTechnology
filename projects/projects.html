ISLLATOR
Bridging the Communication Gap with Indian Sign Language Translation

In India alone, over 1.64 million people live with a speech disability. Globally, speech and communication disorders impact an estimated 5–10% of the population. Individuals with such disabilities often encounter significant challenges, social barriers, and rejection in their daily lives due to limited means of communication.

To address this gap, we present ISLLATOR — an innovative gesture-to-speech translator that empowers individuals with speech disabilities by enabling seamless, real-time communication.

Slide 3 of 6 — How It Works
System Architecture
The ISLLATOR System

ISLLATOR captures and processes Indian Sign Language gestures, translating them into immediate, understandable speech or text using a three-stage pipeline.

📷

Gesture Capture

A standard camera detects and captures ISL signs performed by the user in real time.

🧠

Intelligent Processing

YOLO for gesture detection and OpenCV for image interpretation process each video frame.

🔊

Real-time Output

Translated signs are instantly converted to spoken words via pyttsx3 or displayed as text.

Slide 4 of 6 — Technology
Under the Hood
Technology & Methodology

ISLLATOR leverages a robust tech stack to deliver real-time translation. Each component plays a crucial role in transforming visual signs into audible language.

🐍 Python — The Foundation

Core programming language enabling rapid AI/ML development and seamless integration of all components.

👁️ YOLO — Gesture Detection

"You Only Look Once" — used for efficient, real-time detection of hand gestures and facial expressions with high accuracy.

🎥 OpenCV — Image Processing

Handles pre-processing of video frames, feature extraction from gestures, and sign interpretation.

🔊 pyttsx3 — Voice Output

A cross-platform text-to-speech library that converts translated ISL text into clear, audible speech.

🤖 AI Chatbot — ISL Learning

An intelligent chatbot integration that facilitates ISL learning, answers queries, and provides an interactive educational experience.

Integrated Approach

This integrated architecture ensures a seamless, accurate translation experience while also providing a valuable learning resource for ISL education.

Outlook
Feasibility, Challenges & Solutions
Feasibility & Potential

ISLLATOR's core technology is highly feasible, building upon well-established AI/ML tools. Rapid advancements in these fields provide a solid foundation for improvement.

  • Proven AI/ML models for high accuracy
  • Compatible with widely available cameras
  • Scalable architecture for larger datasets

🗺️ Regional ISL Variations

Challenge: ISL has regional dialects affecting accuracy.
Solution: Expand training datasets with diverse regional ISL signs and adaptive learning algorithms.

⚡ Real-time Accuracy & Latency

Challenge: High accuracy with minimal latency.
Solution: Optimize models for faster inference and explore edge computing.

📱 Hardware Dependency

Challenge: Reliance on phones/computers for processing.
Solution: Develop a dedicated portable offline device for wider accessibility.
