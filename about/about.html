About Us

Building Software That Matters.

CluCore Technology is an emerging Indian tech startup using AI and computer vision to solve real problems — from communication barriers to transit challenges.

Our Mission

Software as a Force for Good.

We started CluCore Technology with a simple belief: the best software doesn't just solve technical puzzles — it changes lives. Our products are born from observing real gaps in society and building technology to bridge them.

"Over 1.64 million people in India live with speech disabilities. They deserve the same tools to communicate as everyone else."

From ISLLATOR — which translates Indian Sign Language into speech in real time — to NavigEye — which ensures commuters never miss their stop — every line of code we write is driven by genuine, measurable impact.

How We Work

Our Approach.

Every product at CluCore follows the same four-phase process from idea to impact.

1

Identify a Real Problem

We start with people, not technology. Each project begins with identifying a verified gap — speech barriers faced by the Deaf community, commuters missing stops — before writing a single line of code.

2

Research & Design

We study existing solutions, understand limitations, and design an approach that is technically feasible, scalable, and genuinely better for the user.

3

Build with the Right Tools

We choose technologies — Python, YOLO, OpenCV, GPS APIs — because they are the best fit, not the most fashionable. Speed, accuracy, and accessibility drive every decision.

4

Measure Impact

A product is only as good as the lives it improves. We track how our tools perform in real-world use and iterate based on feedback from the communities we serve.
